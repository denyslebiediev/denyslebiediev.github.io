How I build.

Three principles behind every app.

  • Private by design.

    Every app asks only for what it needs. No accounts unless they’re essential, and as much as possible stays on your device.

  • Native.

    Swift, SwiftUI, and Apple’s own frameworks, so every app feels at home on iPhone and iPad.

  • For everyone.

    VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and localization ship in the first version, not a later update.

Private by design.

Image Converter Plus converts photos and removes hidden metadata entirely on your device, so your photos never leave it. Money Board Plus needs no account and syncs only through your own private iCloud.

Native.

Interfaces are built in SwiftUI and follow Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines. Features live where you expect them: Image Converter Plus converts photos right from the share sheet, and Money Board Plus keeps your budget in sync through iCloud.

For everyone.

VoiceOver labels, Dynamic Type, and full localization are part of the first release, not a later update. Image Converter Plus is available in more than 50 languages, including Ukrainian.
